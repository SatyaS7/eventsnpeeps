EventsnPeeps Child Safety Standards and CSAE Policy

Last Updated: June 15th, 2026

Our Commitment to Child Safety:

EventsnPeeps is committed to providing a safe environment for all users and maintaining a zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE).
We strictly prohibit any content, behavior, communication, or activity that exploits, endangers, abuses, or sexualizes children. Protecting minors is a core part of our community standards and platform operations.

## Prohibited Content and Conduct

Users may not use EventsnPeeps to: * Create, upload, share, distribute, request, promote, or store Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM). * Groom, manipulate, exploit, or sexually solicit minors. * Engage in sextortion, trafficking, coercion, or exploitation involving children. * Arrange, facilitate, or promote illegal sexual activity involving minors. * Share content that depicts, encourages, glorifies, or promotes child sexual abuse or exploitation. * Use deceptive identities or impersonation to communicate with minors for exploitative purposes. * Attempt to obtain sexually explicit images, videos, or information from minors. * Promote organizations, groups, websites, or services involved in child exploitation. Any violation of these standards may result in immediate account suspension or permanent removal from the platform.

## Reporting Child Safety Concerns

Users who encounter suspected child exploitation, grooming, CSAM, or other child safety concerns should report the content immediately. Reports may include: * User profiles * Messages * Photos * Events * Groups * Comments * Any other content or activity on the platform Reports can be submitted through available in-app reporting tools or by contacting us directly.

## Review and Enforcement

EventsnPeeps reviews reports of child safety violations as quickly as possible. When violations are identified, we may take one or more of the following actions: * Remove content * Restrict platform access * Suspend accounts * Permanently ban users * Preserve evidence where legally required * Cooperate with law enforcement authorities * Report illegal content to appropriate organizations and authorities as required by applicable law We reserve the right to take immediate action when we believe users may be at risk.

## Cooperation with Law Enforcement

EventsnPeeps cooperates with law enforcement agencies and authorized governmental organizations regarding investigations involving child sexual abuse, exploitation, trafficking, or other criminal activities involving minors. Where required by law, we may preserve records and provide information to appropriate authorities.

## Age Requirements

EventsnPeeps is intended only for users who meet the minimum age requirements specified in our Terms of Service and applicable laws. Users who falsely represent their age or create accounts in violation of age requirements may have their accounts removed.

For child safety concerns, CSAE reports, or questions regarding this policy, please contact: **Email:** [support@37labsinc.com](mailto:support@37labsinc.com) Please include sufficient details to help us investigate the reported issue.

## Updates to This Policy

We may update this Child Safety Standards and CSAE Policy from time to time to reflect changes in legal requirements, platform functionality, or safety practices. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
