EventsNPeeps Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 31st, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Thirty Seven Labs Inc. (“Company”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the EventsNPeeps platform, including:

  • the EventsNPeeps website
  • mobile applications
  • related services (the “Service”).

By using the Service you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Information You Provide: When you create an account or use the Service we may collect:

  • name
  • email address
  • phone number
  • password
  • profile information
  • photos
  • interests and preferences
  • messages and communications sent through the Service
  • event descriptions and comments.

We may receive account-related information from authentication providers used to access the Service, such as name, email address, and profile information.

2.1. Activity and Usage Information

We may collect information about how you interact with the Service, such as pages visited, events joined, groups joined, features used, and time spent on the platform.

2.2. Device Information

We may automatically collect device type, operating system, browser type, IP address, and device identifiers.

2.3. Location Information

With your permission we may collect approximate or precise location data to help show relevant events near you.

2.4. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We may use cookies and similar technologies to remember user preferences, improve functionality, analyze usage patterns, and enhance security.

2.5. Payment Information

Paid memberships and subscriptions may be processed through third-party payment providers depending on platform:

  • On Android devices, payments may be processed through Google Play Billing.
  • On iOS devices, payments may be processed through Apple in-app purchase systems.
  • On web platforms, payments may be processed through providers such as Stripe.

We do not store full payment card information on our servers.

2.6. Artificial Intelligence and Interactive Features

When you use our integrated AI feature (conversational assistant), we may collect and store user messages, prompts, queries, questions, system-generated responses, conversation history, and session context. This interaction history is retained to provide continuity across your app sessions.

NOTE: AI-generated responses are automated, and may be inaccurate, incomplete, misleading, or inappropriate and provided for informational purposes only. Users should independently verify important information and should not rely on AI-generated content as legal, financial, medical, mental health, or other professional advice.

3. How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • authenticate users and manage accounts
  • provide and maintain the Service
  • personalize user experiences and AI-driven recommendations
  • improve the quality, relevance, and performance of AI-powered features
  • develop new features and functionality
  • identify bugs, errors, abuse, or misuse of the AI service
  • facilitate events and connections
  • improve platform functionality
  • communicate with users
  • detect fraud and abuse
  • comply with legal obligations.

3.1. Push Notifications

We may send notifications related to events, messages, subscriptions, platform updates, and account activity. Notification preferences may be adjusted in device settings.

4. Subscription and Billing Information

Subscription and billing information associated with memberships may be processed through third-party payment providers. We may receive limited information such as subscription status, transaction identifiers, payment confirmations, and billing metadata.

5. Sharing of Information

We may share information with authentication providers, service providers supporting the platform, payment processors, analytics providers, and legal authorities when required by law.

We use third-party service providers to help operate the Service (cloud hosting, authentication, analytics, notifications, storage, and AI processing). We do not sell user personal data.

5.1. Public Profile & Event Visibility

Certain profile information, events, groups, messages, and content you share through the Service may be visible to other users depending on your settings and use of the Service.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only as long as necessary to operate the Service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Users may request account deletion through the app profile settings or by contacting support@eventsnpeeps.com.

7. Data Security

We implement reasonable security measures including encrypted connections (TLS), access controls, monitoring, logging, and regular backups. However, no system can guarantee absolute security.

8. User Rights

Users may update profile information, control communication preferences, and request account deletion. Depending on your location, additional rights may apply under local privacy laws including rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of personal information.

9. Age Requirement

The Service is not intended for individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

10. International Data Transfers

Information may be processed in the United States and other countries where our service providers operate.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes may be communicated through the Service.

12. Account Deletion Request

You can delete your EventsNPeeps account directly in the app:

Click on the Profile icon in the top-right corner → Select My Profile → Delete Account → Read the confirmation message → Click Confirm to permanently delete your account.

If you are unable to access your account, you may request account deletion by emailing support@eventsnpeeps.com from your registered email address. We permanently remove your account profile and associated user data within 30 days, except where retention is required for legal, security, or compliance obligations.
